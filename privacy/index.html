Reframed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 (mood check-ins now use the PHQ-9)
Effective date: September 28, 2026

Reframed (“the app”) is made by Bowtie Technology (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version: we don't collect any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app saves your mood check-in (PHQ-9) answers and scores, and your thought journal sessions (your thoughts, the thinking patterns you select, and your responses). Starting with version 1.1, if you use the mood journal, the app also saves your mood entries: the date and time, a mood rating from 1 (Very low) to 5 (Great), any emotions you select (for example, sad, anxious, calm, or hopeful), and an optional note you type or dictate. The Trend screen uses these saved entries to show your mood alongside your check-in scores. This information is stored only on your device, inside the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account or sign-in, does not connect to the internet, and contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking tools. It includes no third-party SDKs. No data is collected or transmitted off your device, so we cannot see, access, or recover your entries.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to use voice dictation, the app asks for access to your microphone and to speech recognition. Speech is converted to text on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and the app only listens while you are dictating. If your device can't do on-device recognition, voice input is turned off rather than using a server. You can turn off these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

Backups

Like most apps, the app's data may be included in your device backups (for example, iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple and by your device settings, not by us.

Deleting your data

You can delete journal sessions and individual mood entries in the app, and you can edit mood entries at any time. To remove all app data, including your check-in history, delete the app from your device; this permanently deletes everything the app has stored. Data in existing device backups is governed by your backup settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Health information

The app is a self-reflection tool, not a medical device, and does not provide a diagnosis. Because your entries never leave your device, we do not receive, sell, or share any health information.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, trackers, or third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may process basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the pages, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the updated version on this page and update the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Reframed is for self-reflection only. It is not a medical device, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional care.
