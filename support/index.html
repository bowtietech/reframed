Reframed Support

Hi there! Thanks for using Reframed. If something isn't working or you have an idea, email us at support@bowtietechnology.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Need help right now? If you're thinking about harming yourself, please reach out now. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Elsewhere, call your local emergency number or a local crisis line. Reframed can't contact anyone for you, and we can't respond to emergencies by email.

Common questions

How do I use Reframed?

From the home screen:

  • Mood check-in: tap “Take the PHQ-9 check-in” and answer the 9 questions one at a time. You'll see your score (0 to 27) and range right away. The PHQ-9 looks back over the last 2 weeks, so many people check in about once a week.
  • Trend: after your first check-in, tap “View trend” to see a 90-day chart and your history.
  • Three-column journal: tap “Three-column journal”, then “New session”. List the thoughts on your mind, tap “Start”, then for each thought pick any of the 10 common thinking traps that apply and write a kinder, more balanced response. Sessions save automatically so you can pick up later.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Just open the app and start.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, including check-in scores, journal sessions, and (from version 1.1) mood journal entries and their notes. Reframed doesn't connect to the internet, so we can't see or recover your entries. Your data may be included in your device backups (such as iCloud Backup) if you have those turned on.

Can I take the check-in more than once a day?

You can, but Reframed keeps one entry per day. Retaking it replaces today's score.

Voice dictation isn't working.

Reframed only uses on-device speech recognition to keep your words private. Some devices or languages don't support it, and in that case the mic stays off. You can always type instead. Also check Settings > Reframed and make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are turned on.

How do I delete my data?

To delete a journal session, swipe left on it in the journal list. From version 1.1, you can also swipe on a mood entry to edit or delete it. Individual check-in entries can't be deleted yet (a same-day retake replaces that day's entry). To erase everything, delete the Reframed app from your device; this removes all of its data. Copies in existing device backups follow your backup settings.

Is Reframed a medical app?

No. Reframed is a self-reflection tool. It is not a medical service or medical device, it doesn't diagnose or treat any condition, and it isn't a substitute for professional care. If you're worried about your mood, please talk to someone you trust or a professional.
