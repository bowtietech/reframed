Reframed Support

Hi there! Thanks for using Reframed. If something isn't working or you have an idea, email us at support@bowtietechnology.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Need help right now? If you're thinking about harming yourself, please reach out now. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Elsewhere, call your local emergency number or a local crisis line. Reframed can't contact anyone for you, and we can't respond to emergencies by email.

Common questions

How do I use Reframed?

From the home screen:

  • Daily Reframe Score: tap “Today's Reframe Score” and answer 20 quick questions about today by tapping a face: “Hard today”, “So-so”, or “Doing okay”. You'll get a score from 0 to 100 (higher is better) and a gentle label. It's a personal tracking score, not a clinical measure.
  • PHQ-9 check-in: tap “PHQ-9 check-in (every 2 weeks)” and answer the 9 questions one at a time. You'll see your score (0 to 27) and range right away. The PHQ-9 looks back over the last 2 weeks, so an occasional check-in is enough.
  • Mood journal: tap “Mood journal”, then “New entry”. Pick how you feel, any emotions that fit, and add a note if you like (typed or dictated). Log as often as you want; a 30-day chart shows how your days have been going.
  • Trend: tap “View trend” to see your Reframe Scores and moods together on a 90-day chart, your PHQ-9 check-ins, and your history.
  • Reframe Journal: tap “Reframe Journal”, then “New session”. Write down the automatic thoughts on your mind and tap “Start”. Then take each thought in two steps: pick any of the 11 mental traps you notice (tap the ⓘ icon to read about one), then write a reframe: a kinder, fairer way to see it. Sessions save automatically so you can pick up later.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Just open the app and start.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, including your Reframe Scores, PHQ-9 check-ins, mood journal entries and notes, and Reframe Journal sessions. Reframed doesn't connect to the internet, so we can't see or recover your entries. Your data may be included in your device backups (such as iCloud Backup) if you have those turned on.

Can I take the Reframe Score or PHQ-9 more than once a day?

You can, but Reframed keeps one entry per day for each. Retaking it replaces today's score. The mood journal has no limit: log as many entries as you like.

Voice dictation isn't working.

Reframed only uses on-device speech recognition to keep your words private. Some devices or languages don't support it, and in that case the mic stays off. You can always type instead. Also check Settings > Reframed and make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are turned on.

How do I delete my data?

To delete a Reframe Journal session, swipe left on it in the session list; inside a session you can also swipe left on a single thought. To delete a mood entry, swipe left on it in the mood journal, or tap it to edit it. Individual Reframe Score and PHQ-9 entries can't be deleted yet (a same-day retake replaces that day's entry). To erase everything, delete the Reframed app from your device; this removes all of its data. Copies in existing device backups follow your backup settings.

Is Reframed a medical app?

No. Reframed is a self-reflection tool. It is not a medical service or medical device, it doesn't diagnose or treat any condition, and it isn't a substitute for professional care. If you're worried about your mood, please talk to someone you trust or a professional.
